The Goddess Movement: beyond the existing

What to focus on more

Move
  • Share more short, pre-recorded workout routines that are easy to follow and recreate at home.
  • Create exercise how-to content focused on correct form, common mistakes and simple modifications.
  • Show progressions for more advanced exercises, helping the audience build confidence step by step.
  • Bring back short exercise challenges with 4–5 exercises, encouraging the community to participate and share in the comments.
  • Use challenges as a way to create more interaction and accountability, similar to the energy in TGM community chats.
Live
  • Bring more behind-the-scenes content from TGM classes, events and expert sessions.
  • Turn expert-led Lives into bite-sized educational content, sharing useful insights beyond nutrition.
  • Give more visibility to the experts and practitioners behind TGM, while encouraging them to share their TGM experience with their own audiences.
  • Explore more seasonal wellness and nourishing rituals that fit naturally into everyday life.
  • Expand beyond workouts with more content around wellness, rituals, recovery, mindset and everyday wellbeing.

The Goddess Movement: beyond the existing

What to focus on more

Connect
  • Bring back TGM Spotlight to celebrate the women who make the community what it is.
  • Bring the TGM community chat to social media — challenges, conversations, wellness tips, personal wins and advice that can resonate with a wider audience.
  • Show how TGM girls connect in real life and demonstrate that the community is about more than simply doing workouts together.
  • Share more testimonials, personal stories and UGC to let members speak authentically about what TGM means to them.
  • Showcase the sisterhood, support and personal connections that make TGM feel like a community rather than just a fitness platform.
Inspire
  • Tell more aspirational stories about TGM as a journey back to yourself, rather than focusing only on movement or wellness.
  • Show that there is no single way to experience TGM — everyone can find the tools that resonate with them, whether that is movement, nutrition, wellness, community or simply a moment of support.
  • Celebrate the different ways women connect with TGM and make the platform their own.
  • Create content that communicates the idea that TGM looks different on everyone — and that is exactly what makes it beautiful.
  • Position barre and movement as one expression of the Goddess Movement: moving through life with more love, confidence, connection and harmony.
